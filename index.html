100% On-Device Local Data Storage

SmartProperty prioritizes your financial and property data security. All property listings, tenant contact details, payment histories, and uploaded document attachments are stored directly on your personal device using an encrypted local SQLite database. No external remote servers store your private records.

  • Property & Unit Tracking
  • Tenant & Lease Management
  • Rent & Payment History
  • Expense & Tax Logging
  • PDF & Excel Financial Reports
  • Biometric & PIN App Security
  • Document Storage Vault
  • Local Database Backup & Restore
