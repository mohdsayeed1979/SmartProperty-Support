Data Deletion & Privacy Requests

Clear guidance on removing your personal and property management data from SmartProperty.

1. Understanding Data Ownership & Local Storage

Smart Property-Management App operates on a 100% local, on-device data architecture.

Because all user account credentials, property entries, tenant profiles, financial records, uploaded document attachments, and application settings are stored locally inside an isolated SQLite database on your physical mobile device, we do not maintain a copy of your records on external cloud servers.

ℹ️ Direct User Control
You have complete physical control over your data. You can delete individual records inside the app or remove 100% of your data instantly by clearing the app's local storage or uninstalling the app.

2. How to Delete Data On Your Device

Option A: Delete Specific Records In-App

You can selectively delete any record at any time inside the app:

  • Delete a Property / Unit: Open the property details card, tap Edit or the delete icon, and confirm deletion. This also removes all associated units.
  • Delete a Tenant: Navigate to the tenant profile, tap Delete Tenant, and confirm.
  • Delete Payments or Expenses: Open the target payment or expense ledger item and select Delete.
  • Delete Documents: Open Document Management in Settings, select the target file, and tap Delete.

Option B: Complete App Data Wipe (100% Instant Removal)

To permanently erase all local account records, databases, settings, and attached images:

  • On iOS (iPhone / iPad): Touch and hold the SmartProperty app icon on your Home Screen → Tap Remove App → Select Delete App. This completely wipes all local database files (smart_property.db) and sandbox storage.
  • On Android: Go to Settings → Apps → SmartProperty → Storage & Cache → Tap Clear Storage / Clear Data, or uninstall the app from your Home Screen.

3. Manual Privacy & Data Deletion Requests

Because SmartProperty does not upload or retain your personal records on remote backend servers, uninstalling the app or clearing local data performs a complete and irreversible data deletion without requiring developer intervention.
