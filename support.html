Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Navigate to the Properties tab and tap the floating + button. Enter the property details such as name, property type (Building, Flat, or Land), location address, purchase price, and current market value. You can also upload photos and attach property deeds before saving.
Open the Tenants tab and tap Add Tenant. Select the property and unit number, enter the tenant's name, phone number, and email. Input the monthly rent amount, advance payment, and mandatory lease start and end dates. Save the tenant to start tracking rent dockets.
Select a tenant profile or go to the Rent Payments section in the Tenants tab. Select an open rent period and tap Pay Rent. You can choose to pay the full outstanding balance or enter a custom partial amount. Payment history and remaining dues update automatically.
Open the Reports tab or view a specific tenant profile. Tap the Export PDF or Export Excel buttons. SmartProperty compiles your payment histories, tenant rosters, or expense logs into formatted documents that can be saved to your device or shared via email/messaging.
Go to Settings → Security. Toggle Enable PIN Lock to create a 4-digit PIN, or toggle Enable Biometric Lock to use iOS Face ID / Touch ID or Android Fingerprint unlock whenever the application launches.
Navigate to Settings → Backup & Restore. Tap Create Backup to export a complete copy of your local database and documents to a .zip file. To restore, tap Restore Backup and choose your backup file.
In Settings, select your preferred language (English or Arabic/العربية). The layout will adjust to RTL mode automatically for Arabic. You can also select your preferred currency (e.g., SAR ر.س, USD $, EUR €, AED) for financial displays.

Troubleshooting Guides

1. Camera & Photo Attachment Issues

If you encounter an issue attaching photos or scanning documents:

  • Ensure camera and photo permissions are allowed in your device settings under Settings → SmartProperty → Permissions.
  • Make sure your device has sufficient free storage space for new image files.

2. Local Backup Import Errors

When restoring a database backup:

  • Ensure you select a valid .zip or .json backup archive created by SmartProperty.
  • Restoring a backup replaces current local records with the records in the backup archive. Always create a current backup before restoring.
